Common Questions

What are the school hours?

The regular school day starts at 8:00 AM and ends at 3:00 PM from Monday to Friday. Students can arrive from 7:30 AM for supervised entry, and after-school activities usually run until 4:30 PM. The front office is open during school hours to support families with attendance, schedule questions, and student services. If you need an early pickup, it is best to notify the office in advance so your child can be prepared and the dismissal process stays smooth and safe.

How can parents track homework and progress?

Parents can log in to the parent portal to view homework, attendance, grades, and teacher notes. Progress reports are updated regularly, and families are encouraged to check weekly for the latest updates. We also share progress updates through term reports and parent-teacher meetings. If a parent notices missing work or a drop in performance, they can contact the class teacher to discuss a focused support plan, revision materials, and realistic short-term goals for improvement.

How do I contact a teacher?

The fastest method is through the portal messaging section, where each teacher has a direct contact option. You can also request a call or meeting through the school office during working hours. Please include your student name, class, and the main reason for your message so staff can respond clearly and quickly. Teachers usually respond within one to two school days. For urgent same-day issues, such as transportation or pickup changes, call the office directly for immediate support.

What support is available for students who need extra help?

We provide classroom support, after-school academic sessions, and guided study plans. Teachers identify learning needs early and coordinate with families to create practical improvement goals. Support can include small-group instruction, targeted reading and math practice, and one-to-one check-ins for students who need extra guidance. Our goal is not only to improve grades, but also to build confidence, consistency, and effective learning habits that help students succeed in the long term.

How does attendance and absence reporting work?

If a student is absent, a parent or guardian should notify the school office before 9:00 AM and submit a reason. Attendance is tracked daily, and repeated absences trigger follow-up support from the school team. Students are expected to complete missed classwork, and teachers help prioritize tasks so students can catch up in a manageable way. Since regular attendance strongly supports academic progress and social development, we work closely with families whenever attendance patterns become a concern.

How can students join school clubs and activities?

Students can review available clubs at the start of each term and sign up through their class advisor or activity coordinator. Club schedules, requirements, and participation limits are shared in advance. We offer a range of options, including sports, arts, academic clubs, and service activities, so students can explore interests beyond the classroom. Participation helps students build leadership, teamwork, communication, and confidence while forming stronger connections with the school community.
