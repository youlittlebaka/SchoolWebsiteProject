Common Questions

1. How do I sign in for the first time?

Use your national ID and the default password shared by school administration. After your first sign-in, update your password. If your account is not active yet, contact the office so your role and profile can be confirmed.

2. I forgot my password. What should I do?

Contact administration and request a password reset. For security, identity details may be verified before resetting access. Avoid sharing passwords with classmates or other users.

3. Why can I not see my classroom schedule?

Usually this means your profile is not linked to a class yet, or the class schedule has not been published. Students can only view their own class schedule, while teachers and admins may edit assigned classes.

4. Why is the schedule read-only for me?

Students and parents have read-only access. Editing is available to teachers assigned to that class and administrators. If you are a teacher and still see read-only mode, check class assignment in the class details.

5. How do teachers add homework?

Open the class detail page and use the Add Homework action. Fill in title and description, then submit. Homework items appear immediately in the class homework section.

6. Why does homework show a countdown timer?

Homework is tracked with a 24-hour activity window in the current setup. The timer is a live indicator of how much time is left before the item reaches expiry behavior in the system.

7. Can one class have more than one lesson per day?

Yes. The timetable supports day and period slots. Each class can set lessons for first, second, third, and fourth period. Empty slots can remain empty and do not have to be filled.

8. Who can see student information?

Access depends on role permissions. Teachers can view relevant students, admins have wider access for school operations, and parents are limited to linked student records.

9. Why are some actions blocked with a permission message?

The portal enforces role rules to protect data. If you see a permission warning, your current account role is not allowed to perform that action. Request access changes through administration when appropriate.

10. Can I use the system on mobile?

Yes. The interface is responsive and works on phones and tablets. For timetable editing, landscape mode on smaller screens gives a better table view.

11. How do I report a bug or wrong data?

Send the page link, your account role, and a screenshot to support using the contact channels on the Contact Us page. Include exact steps so the issue can be reproduced quickly.

12. Where can I read more about school goals and values?

Visit the About Us page for mission, educational direction, and partnership approach with families. You can also use Contact Us for direct questions.
